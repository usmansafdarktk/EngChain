===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A symmetric triangular truss has supports A (pin) and C (roller) at the same level, a horizontal distance 2b = 6.4 m apart, and an apex joint B at height h = 2.3 m above the midpoint of AC. The three members are the diagonals AB and BC and the bottom chord AC. A single vertical downward load of P = 54 kN acts at the apex B. Using the method of joints, determine the magnitude of the force in diagonal member AB in kN.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Half-span (b): 3.2 m, apex height (h): 2.3 m
Apex load (P): 54 kN

**Step 1:** Find the support reactions.
By symmetry of the truss and load, each support carries half the load:
Ay = P / 2 = 54 / 2 = 27.0 kN

**Step 2:** Establish the geometry of diagonal AB.
Member length: L_AB = sqrt(h^2 + b^2) = sqrt(2.3^2 + 3.2^2) = 3.941 m
sin(theta) = h / L_AB = 2.3 / 3.941 = 0.5836

**Step 3:** Apply vertical equilibrium at joint A.
Only AB has a vertical component at A (AC is horizontal), so it must balance the upward reaction; AB pushes on the joint, i.e. it is in compression:
F_AB = Ay / sin(theta) = 27.0 / 0.5836 = 46.26 kN (compression)

**Answer:** The force in member AB is 46.26 kN
