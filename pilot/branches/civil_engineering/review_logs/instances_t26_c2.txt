===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A hydraulic jump forms in a rectangular channel carrying a unit discharge of q = 2.524 m^2/s (per meter of width). Measurements show that the upstream (supercritical) depth is 0.29 m. Taking g = 9.81 m/s^2, use the momentum (Belanger) relation to find the sequent depth, and then determine the head loss across the jump in m.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Unit discharge (q): 2.524 m^2/s; the upstream (supercritical) depth is 0.29 m; g = 9.81 m/s^2

**Step 1:** Identify the known side of the jump.
The jump connects a supercritical upstream depth y1 to its subcritical sequent depth y2; here the upstream (supercritical) depth is 0.29 m, so the Belanger relation is applied with the Froude number on the known side.

**Step 2:** Compute the upstream Froude number.
Fr1^2 = q^2 / (g * y1^3) = (2.524)^2 / (9.81 * (0.29)^3) = 26.627

**Step 3:** Apply the Belanger equation for the sequent (downstream) depth.
y2 = (y1/2) * (sqrt(1 + 8*Fr1^2) - 1) = (0.29/2) * (sqrt(1 + 8 * 26.627) - 1) = 1.9762 m

**Step 4:** Compute the head loss across the jump.
dE = (y2 - y1)^3 / (4*y1*y2) = (1.9762 - 0.29)^3 / (4 * 0.29 * 1.9762) = 2.091 m

**Answer:** The head loss across the jump is 2.091 m
