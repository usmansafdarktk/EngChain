===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A highway embankment requires 3170 m^3 of compacted fill with a target void ratio of 0.51. The fill will be constructed from a borrow area whose sand has a moist unit weight of 16.06 kN/m^3 and a moisture content of 8.5%. The specific gravity of the soil solids is 2.67 and the unit weight of water is 9.81 kN/m^3. Determine the volume of borrow soil, in cubic meters, that must be excavated to build the fill.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Required fill volume (V_fill): 3170 m^3
Target fill void ratio (e_fill): 0.51
Borrow moist unit weight (gamma_borrow): 16.06 kN/m^3
Borrow moisture content (w): 8.5% = 0.085
Specific gravity of solids (Gs): 2.67
Unit weight of water (gamma_w): 9.81 kN/m^3

**Step 1:** Compute the required dry unit weight of the compacted fill.
gamma_d,fill = Gs * gamma_w / (1 + e_fill) = 2.67 * 9.81 / (1 + 0.51) = 17.35 kN/m^3

**Step 2:** Compute the dry unit weight of the borrow soil from its moist state.
gamma_d,borrow = gamma_borrow / (1 + w) = 16.06 / (1 + 0.085) = 14.80 kN/m^3

**Step 3:** Compute the weight of solids required in the fill.
The weight of soil solids is conserved from borrow pit to fill:
W_s = gamma_d,fill * V_fill = 17.35 * 3170 = 54999.5 kN

**Step 4:** Compute the required borrow volume.
V_borrow = W_s / gamma_d,borrow = 54999.5 / 14.80 = 3716 m^3

**Answer:** The required borrow volume is 3716 m^3
