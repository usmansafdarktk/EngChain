===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A rectangular channel 4.3 m wide carries a discharge of Q = 11.0 m^3/s at a flow depth of 0.60 m. Taking g = 9.81 m/s^2, determine the Froude number of the flow. In your solution, compute the critical depth and use it to justify whether the flow is subcritical or supercritical.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Width (b): 4.3 m; discharge (Q): 11.0 m^3/s; depth (y): 0.60 m; g = 9.81 m/s^2

**Step 1:** Compute the unit discharge.
q = Q / b = 11.0 / 4.3 = 2.558 m^2/s

**Step 2:** Compute the critical depth.
yc = (q^2 / g)^(1/3) = ((2.558)^2 / 9.81)^(1/3) = 0.874 m

**Step 3:** Compute the Froude number at the actual depth.
Fr = q / (y * sqrt(g*y)) = 2.558 / (0.60 * sqrt(9.81 * 0.60)) = 1.757

**Step 4:** Classify the regime.
Since y = 0.60 m < yc = 0.874 m (equivalently Fr > 1), the flow is supercritical.

**Answer:** The Froude number is 1.757

**Step 3:** Compute the Froude number at the actual depth.

**Step 4:** Classify the regime.
Since y = 3.05 m > yc = 2.179 m (equivalently Fr < 1), the flow is subcritical.

**Answer:** The Froude number is 0.604
