===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A trapezoidal open channel has a bottom width of 4.5 m and side slopes of 2.5H:1V, with a vitrified clay lining (Manning's n = 0.015). It carries uniform flow at a depth of 2.1 m on a slope of S = 0.001. Using the velocity form of Manning's equation in SI units, determine the mean flow velocity in m/s.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Bottom width (b): 4.5 m; depth (y): 2.1 m; side slope z = 2.5
Slope (S): 0.001; lining: vitrified clay, n = 0.015

**Step 1:** Compute the flow area.
A = (b + z*y) * y = (4.5 + 2.5 * 2.1) * 2.1 = 20.475 m^2

**Step 2:** Compute the wetted perimeter and hydraulic radius.
sqrt(1 + z^2) = sqrt(1 + 2.5^2) = 2.6926
P = b + 2*y*sqrt(1 + z^2) = 4.5 + 2 * 2.1 * 2.6926 = 15.809 m
R = A / P = 20.475 / 15.809 = 1.2951 m

**Step 3:** Apply the velocity form of Manning's equation.
R^(2/3) = (1.2951)^(2/3) = 1.1881; S^(1/2) = (0.001)^(1/2) = 0.03162
V = (1/n) * R^(2/3) * S^(1/2) = (1/0.015) * 1.1881 * 0.03162 = 2.505 m/s

**Step 4:** Check the corresponding discharge.
Q = V * A = 2.505 * 20.475 = 51.29 m^3/s, a consistent uniform-flow rate for this channel.

**Answer:** The mean flow velocity is 2.505 m/s
