===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A rectangular open channel is 5.4 m wide and lined with vitrified clay (Manning's n = 0.015). The channel carries uniform flow at a depth of 2.3 m on a longitudinal slope of S = 0.0021. Using Manning's equation in SI units, determine the discharge in m^3/s.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Width (b): 5.4 m; depth (y): 2.3 m
Slope (S): 0.0021; lining: vitrified clay, n = 0.015

**Step 1:** Compute the flow area and wetted perimeter.
A = b * y = 5.4 * 2.3 = 12.420 m^2
P = b + 2y = 5.4 + 2 * 2.3 = 10.00 m

**Step 2:** Compute the hydraulic radius.
R = A / P = 12.420 / 10.00 = 1.2420 m

**Step 3:** Evaluate the Manning terms.
R^(2/3) = (1.2420)^(2/3) = 1.1554
S^(1/2) = (0.0021)^(1/2) = 0.04583

**Step 4:** Apply Manning's equation.
Q = (1/n) * A * R^(2/3) * S^(1/2) = (1/0.015) * 12.420 * 1.1554 * 0.04583 = 43.84 m^3/s

**Answer:** The discharge is 43.84 m^3/s

A rectangular open channel is 4.7 m wide and lined with smooth concrete (Manning's n = 0.012). The channel carries uniform flow at a depth of 1.4 m on a longitudinal slope of S = 0.0024. Using Manning's equation in SI units, determine the discharge in m^3/s.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Width (b): 4.7 m; depth (y): 1.4 m
Slope (S): 0.0024; lining: smooth concrete, n = 0.012

**Step 1:** Compute the flow area and wetted perimeter.
A = b * y = 4.7 * 1.4 = 6.580 m^2
P = b + 2y = 4.7 + 2 * 1.4 = 7.50 m

**Step 2:** Compute the hydraulic radius.
R = A / P = 6.580 / 7.50 = 0.8773 m

**Step 3:** Evaluate the Manning terms.
R^(2/3) = (0.8773)^(2/3) = 0.9164
S^(1/2) = (0.0024)^(1/2) = 0.04899

**Step 4:** Apply Manning's equation.
Q = (1/n) * A * R^(2/3) * S^(1/2) = (1/0.012) * 6.580 * 0.9164 * 0.04899 = 24.62 m^3/s

**Answer:** The discharge is 24.62 m^3/s
