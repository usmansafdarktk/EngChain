===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A beam ABC of constant EI is fixed at A, rests on a roller at B, and is fixed at C. Span AB has length L1 = 5.6 m and span BC has length L2 = 4.5 m. A uniformly distributed load of w = 21 kN/m acts over both spans. There is no support settlement or sidesway. Using the slope-deflection method with the standard sign convention (clockwise end moments positive) and fixed-end moments FEM = -w*L^2/12 (near end) and +w*L^2/12 (far end) for a uniformly loaded span, determine the end moment M_AB at the fixed support A in kN*m.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Spans: L1 = 5.6 m (AB), L2 = 4.5 m (BC); w = 21 kN/m on both; theta_A = theta_C = 0 (fixed ends), no sidesway.

**Step 1:** Compute the fixed-end moments for each span.
FEM_AB = -w*L1^2/12 = -21 * (5.6)^2 / 12 = -54.88 kN*m
FEM_BA = +w*L1^2/12 = 54.88 kN*m
FEM_BC = -w*L2^2/12 = -21 * (4.5)^2 / 12 = -35.44 kN*m
FEM_CB = +w*L2^2/12 = 35.44 kN*m

**Step 2:** Write the slope-deflection equations with theta_A = theta_C = 0, letting X = EI*theta_B.
M_BA = (4/L1)*X + FEM_BA = (4/5.6)*X + 54.88
M_BC = (4/L2)*X + FEM_BC = (4/4.5)*X + (-35.44)

**Step 3:** Enforce moment equilibrium of joint B.
M_BA + M_BC = 0:
(1.6032)*X = -19.44
X = -19.44 / 1.6032 = -12.126 kN*m^2  (EI*theta_B)

**Step 4:** Back-substitute into the slope-deflection equation for the moment at A.
M_AB = (2/L1)*X + FEM_AB = (2/5.6) * -12.126 + (-54.88) = -59.21 kN*m

**Answer:** The end moment at support A is -59.21 kN*m
