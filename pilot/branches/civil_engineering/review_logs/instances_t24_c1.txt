===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
Uniform flow of Q = 21.14 m^3/s occurs in a rectangular channel of width b = 4.8 m, lined with vitrified clay (Manning's n = 0.015) on a slope of S = 0.0018. Determine the normal depth of flow in m, iterating until the depth changes by less than 0.002 m.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Q = 21.14 m^3/s; a rectangular channel of width b = 4.8 m; n = 0.015; S = 0.0018

**Step 1:** Form the required section factor from Manning's equation.
A*R^(2/3) = Q*n / S^(1/2) = 21.14 * 0.015 / 0.04243 = 7.473

**Step 2:** Set up the trial-and-interpolation scheme.
With A = b*y and P = b + 2y, the section factor A*R^(2/3) increases monotonically with depth. Define g(y) = A*R^(2/3) - K, evaluate it at two trial depths (1.0000 m and 1.5000 m), and update by linear interpolation between successive trials until the depth changes by less than 0.002 m.

**Step 3:** Iterate.
Trials: g(y) = A*R^(2/3) - K. At y = 1.0000 m, g = -3.668; at y = 1.5000 m, g = -0.647.
Update 1: y_next = 1.5000 - (-0.647) * (1.5000 - 1.0000) / ((-0.647) - (-3.668)) = 1.6071 m
Update 2: y_next = 1.6071 - (0.047) * (1.6071 - 1.5000) / ((0.047) - (-0.647)) = 1.5998 m
Update 3: y_next = 1.5998 - (-0.000) * (1.5998 - 1.6071) / ((-0.000) - (0.047)) = 1.5998 m

**Step 4:** State the converged normal depth.
The last change is below the tolerance, so yn = 1.600 m

**Answer:** The normal depth is 1.600 m
