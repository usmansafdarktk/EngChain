===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
Water flows at a unit discharge of q = 3.241 m^2/s in a wide rectangular channel at an approach depth of y1 = 1.47 m (subcritical). The bed rises gradually over a smooth hump. Taking g = 9.81 m/s^2 and neglecting energy losses, determine the maximum hump height, in m, for which the flow passes without choking (i.e., without forcing critical conditions on the hump).
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Unit discharge (q): 3.241 m^2/s; approach depth (y1): 1.47 m; g = 9.81 m/s^2

**Step 1:** Compute the approach specific energy.
E1 = y1 + q^2/(2*g*y1^2) = 1.47 + (3.241)^2 / (2 * 9.81 * (1.47)^2) = 1.718 m

**Step 2:** Compute the critical depth and minimum specific energy.
yc = (q^2/g)^(1/3) = ((3.241)^2 / 9.81)^(1/3) = 1.023 m
Ec = 1.5 * yc = 1.5 * 1.023 = 1.534 m

**Step 3:** The flow chokes when the energy over the hump drops to Ec, so the maximum bed rise equals the energy margin.
dz_max = E1 - Ec = 1.718 - 1.534 = 0.184 m

**Answer:** The maximum hump height without choking is 0.184 m
