===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A simply supported beam AB (pin at A, roller at B) has a span of L = 12.6 m. A two-axle vehicle crosses the beam: the front axle carries 64 kN and the rear axle carries 128 kN, with a fixed axle spacing of s = 3.9 m. Both axles remain on the span. Using the influence line for the vertical reaction at A, determine the maximum value of that reaction in kN.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Span (L): 12.6 m
Axle loads: front 64 kN, rear 128 kN; spacing s = 3.9 m

**Step 1:** Construct the influence line for the reaction at A.
For a unit load at distance x from A, R_A = (L - x)/L: the ordinate is 1 at A (x = 0) and decreases linearly to 0 at B (x = L).

**Step 2:** Position the axles for the maximum effect.
Since the ordinates decrease away from A, the heavier axle (the rear axle, 128 kN) is placed directly over A, and the lighter axle (64 kN) then sits at x = s = 3.9 m.

**Step 3:** Read the influence-line ordinates under each axle.
Under the heavy axle: y = 1.0000 (at A)
Under the light axle: y = (L - s)/L = (12.6 - 3.9) / 12.6 = 0.6905

**Step 4:** Sum load times ordinate.
R_A,max = 128 * 1.0000 + 64 * 0.6905 = 172.19 kN

**Answer:** The maximum reaction at A is 172.19 kN
