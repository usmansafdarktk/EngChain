===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A strip footing 1.8 m wide is founded at a depth of 1.1 m in a deep deposit of loose to medium sand with unit weight 16.5 kN/m^3, effective cohesion c' = 0 kPa, and drained friction angle phi' = 30 degrees. Field tests indicate a relative density of 44%. The groundwater table is well below the footing. Terzaghi's strip-footing equations are qu = c'*Nc + q*Nq + 0.5*gamma*B*Ngamma for general shear failure and qu = (2/3)c'*N'c + q*N'q + 0.5*gamma*B*N'gamma for local shear failure, where q = gamma*Df. For phi' = 30 degrees: Nc = 37.16, Nq = 22.46, Ngamma = 19.13; N'c = 18.99, N'q = 8.31, N'gamma = 4.39. Assume general shear failure governs when the relative density exceeds 70%; otherwise local shear failure governs. Determine the allowable bearing capacity in kPa for a factor of safety of 3.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Footing width (B): 1.8 m, depth (Df): 1.1 m
Soil: gamma = 16.5 kN/m^3, c' = 0 kPa, phi' = 30 deg
Site data: Field tests indicate a relative density of 44%.
General-shear factors: Nc = 37.16, Nq = 22.46, Ngamma = 19.13
Local-shear factors: N'c = 18.99, N'q = 8.31, N'gamma = 4.39
Factor of safety (FS): 3

**Step 1:** Decide the failure mode.
Since the relative density is 44%, which is not greater than 70%, local shear failure governs.

**Step 2:** Select the governing equation and factors.
qu = (2/3)c'*N'c + q*N'q + 0.5*gamma*B*N'gamma, with N'c = 18.99, N'q = 8.31, N'gamma = 4.39. The mobilized cohesion is reduced: (2/3)c' = (2/3) * 0 = 0.00 kPa.

**Step 3:** Compute the effective surcharge at the footing base.
q = gamma * Df = 16.5 * 1.1 = 18.15 kPa

**Step 4:** Evaluate the three terms of the selected equation.
Cohesion term: (2/3)c' * N'c = 0.00 * 18.99 = 0.0 kPa
Surcharge term: q * N'q = 18.15 * 8.31 = 150.8 kPa
Width term: 0.5 * gamma * B * N'gamma = 0.5 * 16.5 * 1.8 * 4.39 = 65.2 kPa

**Step 5:** Sum the terms to obtain the ultimate bearing capacity.
qu = 0.0 + 150.8 + 65.2 = 216.0 kPa

**Step 6:** Apply the factor of safety.
q_all = qu / FS = 216.0 / 3 = 72.0 kPa

**Answer:** The allowable bearing capacity is 72.0 kPa
