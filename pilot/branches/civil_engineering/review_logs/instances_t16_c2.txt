===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A simply supported steel beam consists of a W24X76 (SI designation W610X113) section (moment of inertia I = 874.0 x 10^6 mm^4, modulus of elasticity E = 200 GPa) spanning L = 11.7 m. The beam carries a concentrated load of 153.7 kN at midspan. Determine the maximum deflection of the beam in millimeters.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Section: W24X76 (SI designation W610X113), I = 874.0 x 10^6 mm^4
E = 200 GPa; span L = 11.7 m
Load: a concentrated load of 153.7 kN at midspan

**Step 1:** Select the deflection formula.
For a simply supported beam with a concentrated load at midspan, the maximum (midspan) deflection is:
delta = P*L^3 / (48*E*I)

**Step 2:** Assemble consistent SI units.
E = 200 GPa = 200000000 kN/m^2
I = 874.0 x 10^6 mm^4 = 8.7400e-04 m^4
Span L = 11.7 m

**Step 3:** Substitute and evaluate.
delta = 153.7 * (11.7)^3 / (48 * 200000000 * 8.7400e-04) = 0.02934 m

**Step 4:** Express in millimeters.
delta = 0.02934 * 1000 = 29.3 mm

**Answer:** The maximum deflection is 29.3 mm

===== SEED 102 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A simply supported steel beam consists of a W12X26 section (moment of inertia I = 204 in^4, modulus of elasticity E = 29000 ksi) spanning L = 20 ft = 240 in. The beam carries a uniformly distributed load of 1.21 kip/ft over the full span. Determine the maximum deflection of the beam in inches.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Section: W12X26, I = 204 in^4
E = 29000 ksi; span L = 20 ft = 240 in
Load: a uniformly distributed load of 1.21 kip/ft over the full span

**Step 1:** Select the deflection formula.
For a simply supported beam under a full-span uniform load, the maximum (midspan) deflection is:
delta = 5*w*L^4 / (384*E*I)

**Step 2:** Assemble consistent US customary units (kips and inches).
E = 29000 ksi
I = 204 in^4
Span L = 20 ft = 240 in
w = 1.21 kip/ft = 0.10083 kip/in

**Step 3:** Substitute and evaluate.
delta = 5 * 0.10083 * (240)^4 / (384 * 29000 * 204) = 0.736 in

**Answer:** The maximum deflection is 0.736 in
