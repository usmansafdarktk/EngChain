===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A detention basin behaves as a linear reservoir whose storage is proportional to its outflow, S = k*O with k = 5040 s. An inflow hydrograph rises through I1 = 3.6, I2 = 13.3, and I3 = 20.8 m^3/s at successive times separated by dt = 1800 s, and the outflow at the first time is O1 = 1.6 m^3/s. Starting from the level-pool continuity equation (I_j + I_j+1)/2 * dt - (O_j + O_j+1)/2 * dt = S_j+1 - S_j, derive the working equation for the end-of-interval outflow and apply it over both intervals to determine the outflow O3, in m^3/s, at the end of the second interval.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Storage relation: S = k*O, k = 5040 s; dt = 1800 s
Inflows: I1 = 3.6, I2 = 13.3, I3 = 20.8 m^3/s; initial outflow O1 = 1.6 m^3/s

**Step 1:** Substitute the storage relation into continuity and expand.
(I_j + I_j+1)/2 * dt - (O_j + O_j+1)/2 * dt = k*(O_j+1 - O_j)
(I_j + I_j+1)*dt/2 - O_j*dt/2 - O_j+1*dt/2 = k*O_j+1 - k*O_j

**Step 2:** Collect the unknown O_j+1 on one side.
(I_j + I_j+1)*dt/2 + O_j*(k - dt/2) = O_j+1*(k + dt/2)
O_j+1 = [ (I_j + I_j+1)*dt/2 + O_j*(k - dt/2) ] / (k + dt/2)
Here k - dt/2 = 5040 - 900.0 = 4140.0 s and k + dt/2 = 5040 + 900.0 = 5940.0 s.

**Step 3:** Route the first interval (I1 -> I2).
(I1 + I2)*dt/2 = (3.6 + 13.3) * 900.0 = 15210.0 m^3
O1*(k - dt/2) = 1.6 * 4140.0 = 6624.0 m^3
O2 = (15210.0 + 6624.0) / 5940.0 = 3.68 m^3/s

**Step 4:** Route the second interval (I2 -> I3), using the O2 just computed.
(I2 + I3)*dt/2 = (13.3 + 20.8) * 900.0 = 30690.0 m^3
O2*(k - dt/2) = 3.68 * 4140.0 = 15235.2 m^3
O3 = (30690.0 + 15235.2) / 5940.0 = 7.73 m^3/s

**Step 5:** Confirm the routed outflow behaves physically.
O1 = 1.6 < O2 = 3.68 < O3 = 7.73 m^3/s, all below the peak inflow I3 = 20.8 m^3/s — a rising, attenuated outflow as expected for a detention basin.

**Answer:** The outflow at the end of the second interval is 7.73 m^3/s
