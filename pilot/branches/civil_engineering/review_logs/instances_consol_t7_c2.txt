===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A soil profile consists of, from the ground surface down: 2.4 m of moist sand (unit weight 17.9 kN/m^3) above the water table; 2.6 m of saturated sand (saturated unit weight 19.7 kN/m^3); and a 4.9 m thick layer of normally consolidated clay (saturated unit weight 18.14 kN/m^3, initial void ratio 1.06, liquid limit 56%). The water table is at the bottom of the moist sand layer. A rectangular raft foundation, 11.5 m by 15.4 m in plan, applies a uniform net pressure of 267 kPa at the ground surface. Using the 2:1 stress distribution method for the stress increase at the middle of the clay layer, the Skempton correlation Cc = 0.009(LL - 10), and a unit weight of water of 9.81 kN/m^3, determine the primary consolidation settlement of the clay layer in millimeters.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Moist sand: z1 = 2.4 m, gamma = 17.9 kN/m^3
Saturated sand: z2 = 2.6 m, gamma_sat = 19.7 kN/m^3
Clay: Hc = 4.9 m, gamma_sat = 18.14 kN/m^3, e0 = 1.06, LL = 56%
Raft: 11.5 m x 15.4 m, net pressure q0 = 267 kPa at the surface
Unit weight of water (gamma_w): 9.81 kN/m^3

**Step 1:** Estimate the compression index from the Skempton correlation.
Cc = 0.009 * (LL - 10) = 0.009 * (56 - 10) = 0.414

**Step 2:** Compute the submerged unit weights below the water table.
Sand: gamma'_sand = 19.7 - 9.81 = 9.89 kN/m^3
Clay: gamma'_clay = 18.14 - 9.81 = 8.33 kN/m^3

**Step 3:** Compute the initial effective stress at the middle of the clay layer.
sigma'0 = 17.9 * 2.4 + 9.89 * 2.6 + 8.33 * 2.45 = 89.1 kPa

**Step 4:** Compute the depth from the raft to the middle of the clay layer.
z = z1 + z2 + Hc/2 = 2.4 + 2.6 + 2.45 = 7.45 m

**Step 5:** Compute the stress increase at that depth by the 2:1 method.
The load spreads over an area (B + z) by (L + z):
delta_sigma = q0 * B * L / ((B + z) * (L + z)) = 267 * 11.5 * 15.4 / ((11.5 + 7.45) * (15.4 + 7.45)) = 109.2 kPa

**Step 6:** Compute the final effective stress at the clay mid-depth.
sigma'f = sigma'0 + delta_sigma = 89.1 + 109.2 = 198.3 kPa

**Step 7:** Compute the logarithmic stress-ratio term.
log10(sigma'f / sigma'0) = log10(198.3 / 89.1) = 0.3474

**Step 8:** Compute the primary consolidation settlement.
Sc = Cc * Hc / (1 + e0) * log10(sigma'f/sigma'0) = 0.414 * 4.9 / (1 + 1.06) * 0.3474 = 0.3421 m

**Step 9:** Convert to millimeters.
Sc = 0.3421 * 1000 = 342 mm

**Answer:** The primary consolidation settlement is 342 mm
