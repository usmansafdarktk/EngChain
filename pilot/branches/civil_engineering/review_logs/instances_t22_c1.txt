===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A trapezoidal open channel has a bottom width of 4.5 m and side slopes of 2.5H:1V, and is lined with vitrified clay (Manning's n = 0.015). It carries uniform flow at a depth of 2.1 m on a slope of S = 0.0015. Using Manning's equation in SI units, determine the discharge in m^3/s.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Bottom width (b): 4.5 m; depth (y): 2.1 m; side slope z = 2.5
Slope (S): 0.0015; lining: vitrified clay, n = 0.015

**Step 1:** Compute the flow area.
A = (b + z*y) * y = (4.5 + 2.5 * 2.1) * 2.1 = 20.475 m^2

**Step 2:** Compute the wetted perimeter and hydraulic radius.
sqrt(1 + z^2) = sqrt(1 + 2.5^2) = 2.6926
P = b + 2*y*sqrt(1 + z^2) = 4.5 + 2 * 2.1 * 2.6926 = 15.809 m
R = A / P = 20.475 / 15.809 = 1.2951 m

**Step 3:** Evaluate the Manning terms.
R^(2/3) = (1.2951)^(2/3) = 1.1881
S^(1/2) = (0.0015)^(1/2) = 0.03873

**Step 4:** Apply Manning's equation.
Q = (1/n) * A * R^(2/3) * S^(1/2) = (1/0.015) * 20.475 * 1.1881 * 0.03873 = 62.81 m^3/s

**Answer:** The discharge is 62.81 m^3/s

===== SEED 102 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A trapezoidal open channel has a bottom width of 3.9 m and side slopes of 2.0H:1V, and is lined with smooth concrete (Manning's n = 0.012). It carries uniform flow at a depth of 1.3 m on a slope of S = 0.0018. Using Manning's equation in SI units, determine the discharge in m^3/s.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Bottom width (b): 3.9 m; depth (y): 1.3 m; side slope z = 2.0
Slope (S): 0.0018; lining: smooth concrete, n = 0.012

**Step 1:** Compute the flow area.
A = (b + z*y) * y = (3.9 + 2.0 * 1.3) * 1.3 = 8.450 m^2

**Step 2:** Compute the wetted perimeter and hydraulic radius.
sqrt(1 + z^2) = sqrt(1 + 2.0^2) = 2.2361
P = b + 2*y*sqrt(1 + z^2) = 3.9 + 2 * 1.3 * 2.2361 = 9.714 m
R = A / P = 8.450 / 9.714 = 0.8699 m

**Step 3:** Evaluate the Manning terms.
R^(2/3) = (0.8699)^(2/3) = 0.9113
S^(1/2) = (0.0018)^(1/2) = 0.04243

**Step 4:** Apply Manning's equation.
Q = (1/n) * A * R^(2/3) * S^(1/2) = (1/0.012) * 8.450 * 0.9113 * 0.04243 = 27.23 m^3/s

**Answer:** The discharge is 27.23 m^3/s
