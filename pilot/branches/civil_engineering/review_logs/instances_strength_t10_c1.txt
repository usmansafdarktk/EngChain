===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A strip footing 1.7 m wide is founded at a depth of 1.2 m in compacted silty soil with unit weight 19.3 kN/m^3, effective cohesion c' = 20 kPa, and drained friction angle phi' = 25 degrees. The groundwater table is well below the footing. Using Terzaghi's bearing capacity equation for general shear failure with Nc = 25.13, Nq = 12.72, and Ngamma = 10.88, determine the allowable bearing capacity in kPa for a factor of safety of 3.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Footing width (B): 1.7 m, depth (Df): 1.2 m
Soil: gamma = 19.3 kN/m^3, c' = 20 kPa, phi' = 25 deg
Bearing capacity factors: Nc = 25.13, Nq = 12.72, Ngamma = 10.88
Factor of safety (FS): 3

**Step 1:** Compute the effective surcharge at the footing base.
q = gamma * Df = 19.3 * 1.2 = 23.16 kPa

**Step 2:** Evaluate the three terms of Terzaghi's equation for a strip footing.
Cohesion term: c' * Nc = 20 * 25.13 = 502.6 kPa
Surcharge term: q * Nq = 23.16 * 12.72 = 294.6 kPa
Width term: 0.5 * gamma * B * Ngamma = 0.5 * 19.3 * 1.7 * 10.88 = 178.5 kPa

**Step 3:** Sum the terms to obtain the ultimate bearing capacity.
qu = 502.6 + 294.6 + 178.5 = 975.7 kPa

**Step 4:** Apply the factor of safety.
q_all = qu / FS = 975.7 / 3 = 325.2 kPa

**Answer:** The allowable bearing capacity is 325.2 kPa
