===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A catchment consists of streets: gravel on hydrologic soil group B, for which TR-55 gives a curve number CN = 85. A design storm delivers P = 5.8 inches of rainfall. Using the SCS curve-number method with S = 1000/CN - 10 and Q = (P - 0.2S)^2 / (P + 0.8S), all quantities in inches, determine the direct runoff depth Q in inches.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Land use: streets: gravel; HSG B; CN = 85
Storm depth (P): 5.8 in

**Step 1:** Compute the potential maximum retention.
S = 1000/CN - 10 = 1000/85 - 10 = 1.76 in

**Step 2:** Check the initial abstraction.
Ia = 0.2*S = 0.2 * 1.76 = 0.35 in; since P = 5.8 in > Ia, runoff occurs and the runoff equation applies.

**Step 3:** Compute the direct runoff depth.
Q = (P - 0.2S)^2 / (P + 0.8S) = (5.8 - 0.35)^2 / (5.8 + 0.8 * 1.76) = 4.12 in

**Answer:** The direct runoff depth is 4.12 in
