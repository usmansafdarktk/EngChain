===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
During a design storm with rainfall intensity i = 52 mm/h, determine the peak discharge in m^3/s from a 13.9 ha catchment consisting of a light industrial area with a runoff coefficient of C = 0.64 (within its HEC-22 Table 3-1 range), using the rational method in SI form (Q = C*i*A/360 with i in mm/h and A in hectares).
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Rainfall intensity (i): 52 mm/h
Catchment: a 13.9 ha catchment consisting of a light industrial area with a runoff coefficient of C = 0.64 (within its HEC-22 Table 3-1 range)

**Step 1:** State the rational formula in SI form.
Q = C * i * A / 360, with Q in m^3/s, i in mm/h, and A in ha.

**Step 2:** Substitute the given values.
Q = 0.64 * 52 * 13.9 / 360

**Step 3:** Evaluate the peak discharge.
Q = 1.28 m^3/s

**Answer:** The peak discharge is 1.28 m^3/s

===== SEED 103 =====
--- QUESTION ---
During a design storm with rainfall intensity i = 68 mm/h, determine the peak discharge in m^3/s from a catchment with two land uses: 4.1 ha of multi-unit detached residential area (C = 0.42) and 6.4 ha of light industrial area (C = 0.68), the runoff coefficients taken from HEC-22 Table 3-1, using the rational method in SI form (Q = C*i*A/360 with i in mm/h and A in hectares).
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Rainfall intensity (i): 68 mm/h
Catchment: a catchment with two land uses: 4.1 ha of multi-unit detached residential area (C = 0.42) and 6.4 ha of light industrial area (C = 0.68), the runoff coefficients taken from HEC-22 Table 3-1

**Step 1:** Compute the area-weighted composite runoff coefficient.
C_w = (C1*A1 + C2*A2) / (A1 + A2) = (0.42 * 4.1 + 0.68 * 6.4) / 10.5 = 0.578

**Step 2:** State the rational formula in SI form.
Q = C_w * i * A / 360, with Q in m^3/s, i in mm/h, and A in ha.

**Step 3:** Substitute and evaluate.
Q = 0.578 * 68 * 10.5 / 360 = 1.15 m^3/s

**Answer:** The peak discharge is 1.15 m^3/s
