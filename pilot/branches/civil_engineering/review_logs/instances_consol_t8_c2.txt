===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A 4.3 m thick saturated clay layer in the field is bounded by sand layers above and below the clay (double drainage). In the laboratory, a 25 mm thick specimen of the same clay, drained on both faces, reaches 50% average consolidation in 38 minutes. Using the time-factor relations Tv = (pi/4)(U/100)^2 for U <= 60% and Tv = 1.781 - 0.933*log10(100 - U) for U > 60%, determine the time in years required for the field layer to reach an average degree of consolidation of 90%. (Use 525,600 minutes per year.)
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Field clay thickness (Hc): 4.3 m, double drainage
Lab specimen: 25 mm thick, drained on both faces, t50 = 38 min
Target field degree of consolidation (U): 90%

**Step 1:** Determine the laboratory drainage path and its time factor.
The doubly drained 25 mm specimen has H_dr,lab = 25 / 2 = 12.5 mm. At 50% consolidation (U <= 60%):
T50 = (pi/4) * (0.50)^2 = 0.1963

**Step 2:** Determine the field drainage path.
With permeable sand above and below, the clay drains from both faces:
H_dr,field = Hc / 2 = 4.3 / 2 = 2.15 m

**Step 3:** Compute the field time factor for U = 90%.
Since U > 60%, Tv = 1.781 - 0.933 * log10(100 - U) = 1.781 - 0.933 * log10(10) = 0.8480

**Step 4:** Form the drainage-path ratio between field and lab.
H_dr,field / H_dr,lab = 2.15 m / 12.5 mm = 2150 / 12.5 = 172.0

**Step 5:** Apply similitude of the time factor (Tv = cv*t/H_dr^2, same cv in lab and field).
t_field = t50 * (Tv / T50) * (H_dr,field / H_dr,lab)^2 = 38 * (0.8480 / 0.1963) * (172.0)^2 = 4856418 min

**Step 6:** Convert to years.
t_field = 4856418 / 525600 = 9.24 years

**Answer:** The required field consolidation time is 9.24 years
