===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A detention basin behaves as a linear reservoir whose storage is proportional to its outflow, S = k*O with k = 5040 s. During a routing interval of dt = 1800 s, the inflow rises from I1 = 3.6 m^3/s to I2 = 18.1 m^3/s, and the outflow at the start of the interval is O1 = 2.1 m^3/s. Starting from the level-pool continuity equation (I1 + I2)/2 * dt - (O1 + O2)/2 * dt = S2 - S1, derive the working equation for the end-of-interval outflow and determine O2 in m^3/s.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Storage relation: S = k*O, k = 5040 s; dt = 1800 s
Inflows: I1 = 3.6, I2 = 18.1 m^3/s; initial outflow O1 = 2.1 m^3/s

**Step 1:** Write continuity over the interval and substitute the storage relation.
(I1 + I2)/2 * dt - (O1 + O2)/2 * dt = S2 - S1 = k*(O2 - O1)

**Step 2:** Collect the unknown O2 on one side.
(I1 + I2)*dt/2 + O1*(k - dt/2) = O2*(k + dt/2)
O2 = [ (I1 + I2)*dt/2 + O1*(k - dt/2) ] / (k + dt/2)

**Step 3:** Evaluate the terms.
(I1 + I2)*dt/2 = (3.6 + 18.1) * 900.0 = 19530.0 m^3
O1*(k - dt/2) = 2.1 * (5040 - 900.0) = 8694.0 m^3
k + dt/2 = 5040 + 900.0 = 5940.0 s

**Step 4:** Compute the end-of-interval outflow.
O2 = (19530.0 + 8694.0) / 5940.0 = 4.75 m^3/s

**Answer:** The outflow at the end of the interval is 4.75 m^3/s
