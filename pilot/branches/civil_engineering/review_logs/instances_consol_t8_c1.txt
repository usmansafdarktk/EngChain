===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A 4.3 m thick saturated clay layer is bounded by sand layers above and below the clay (double drainage). The layer will consolidate under a wide surcharge, and its coefficient of consolidation is 7.5 m^2/yr. Determine the time required, in years, for the layer to reach an average degree of consolidation of 90%.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Clay layer thickness (Hc): 4.3 m
Drainage condition: double drainage
Coefficient of consolidation (cv): 7.5 m^2/yr
Target average degree of consolidation (U): 90%

**Step 1:** Determine the drainage path.
With permeable sand above and below, the clay drains from both faces, so the maximum drainage path is half the layer thickness:
H_dr = Hc / 2 = 4.3 / 2 = 2.15 m

**Step 2:** Compute the time factor for U = 90%.
Since U > 60%, Tv = 1.781 - 0.933 * log10(100 - U) = 1.781 - 0.933 * log10(10) = 0.8480

**Step 3:** Solve the time-factor equation for the required time.
t = Tv * H_dr^2 / cv = 0.8480 * (2.15)^2 / 7.5 = 0.52 years

**Answer:** The required consolidation time is 0.52 years

===== SEED 102 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A 2.6 m thick saturated clay layer is bounded by a sand layer above the clay and impervious rock below it (single drainage). The layer will consolidate under a wide surcharge, and its coefficient of consolidation is 4.9 m^2/yr. Determine the time required, in years, for the layer to reach an average degree of consolidation of 65%.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Clay layer thickness (Hc): 2.6 m
Drainage condition: single drainage
Coefficient of consolidation (cv): 4.9 m^2/yr
Target average degree of consolidation (U): 65%

**Step 1:** Determine the drainage path.
With impervious rock below, the clay drains through its top face only, so the maximum drainage path is the full layer thickness:
H_dr = Hc = 2.6 m, i.e. H_dr = 2.60 m

**Step 2:** Compute the time factor for U = 65%.
Since U > 60%, Tv = 1.781 - 0.933 * log10(100 - U) = 1.781 - 0.933 * log10(35) = 0.3404

**Step 3:** Solve the time-factor equation for the required time.
t = Tv * H_dr^2 / cv = 0.3404 * (2.60)^2 / 4.9 = 0.47 years

**Answer:** The required consolidation time is 0.47 years
