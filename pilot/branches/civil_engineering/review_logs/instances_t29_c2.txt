===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A catchment consists of a gravel-road corridor on hydrologic soil group B, for which TR-55 gives a curve number CN = 85. A design storm delivers P = 148 mm of rainfall. The SCS runoff equation is valid only with all depths in inches (1 in = 25.4 mm): S = 1000/CN - 10 and Q = (P - 0.2S)^2 / (P + 0.8S). Determine the direct runoff depth in millimeters.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Land use: a gravel-road corridor; HSG B; CN = 85
Storm depth (P): 148 mm

**Step 1:** Convert the storm depth to inches (the SCS equation is inch-bound).
P = 148 / 25.4 = 5.83 in

**Step 2:** Compute the potential maximum retention.
S = 1000/CN - 10 = 1000/85 - 10 = 1.765 in

**Step 3:** Check the initial abstraction.
Ia = 0.2*S = 0.2 * 1.765 = 0.353 in; since P = 5.83 in > Ia, runoff occurs and the runoff equation applies.

**Step 4:** Compute the direct runoff depth in inches.
Q = (P - 0.2S)^2 / (P + 0.8S) = (5.83 - 0.353)^2 / (5.83 + 0.8 * 1.765) = 4.142 in

**Step 5:** Convert the runoff back to millimeters.
Q = 4.142 * 25.4 = 105.2 mm

**Answer:** The direct runoff depth is 105.2 mm
