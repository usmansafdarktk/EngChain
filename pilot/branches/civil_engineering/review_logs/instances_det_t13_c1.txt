===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A simply supported beam AB (pin at A, roller at B) has a span of L = 9.5 m. It carries a uniformly distributed load of 6 kN/m over the entire span and a concentrated load of 27 kN at a = 6.3 m from A. Determine the internal bending moment, in kN*m, at the section located x = 7.4 m from support A.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Span (L): 9.5 m; UDL (w): 6 kN/m over the full span
Point load (P): 27 kN at a = 6.3 m from A
Section location: x = 7.4 m from A

**Step 1:** Find the support reactions.
Sum(M_A) = 0: By = (P * a + w * L * L/2) / L = (27 * 6.3 + 6 * 9.5 * 4.75) / 9.5 = 46.41 kN
Sum(F_y) = 0: Ay = P + w * L - By = 27 + 6 * 9.5 - 46.41 = 37.59 kN

**Step 2:** Cut the beam at x = 7.4 m and take the left free body.
The forces crossing the cut are the reaction Ay, the distributed load over the length x, and the point load P (since x = 7.4 m > a = 6.3 m).

**Step 3:** Sum moments about the cut to find the internal moment (sagging positive).
M = Ay * x - w * x^2 / 2 - P * (x - a)
M = 37.59 * 7.4 - 6 * (7.4)^2 / 2 - 27 * (7.4 - 6.3) = 84.19 kN*m

**Answer:** The internal bending moment at the section is 84.19 kN*m

===== SEED 102 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A simply supported beam AB (pin at A, roller at B) has a span of L = 6.9 m. It carries a uniformly distributed load of 6 kN/m over the entire span and a concentrated load of 54 kN at a = 3.1 m from A. Determine the internal bending moment, in kN*m, at the section located x = 1.6 m from support A.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Span (L): 6.9 m; UDL (w): 6 kN/m over the full span
Point load (P): 54 kN at a = 3.1 m from A
Section location: x = 1.6 m from A

**Step 1:** Find the support reactions.
Sum(M_A) = 0: By = (P * a + w * L * L/2) / L = (54 * 3.1 + 6 * 6.9 * 3.45) / 6.9 = 44.96 kN
Sum(F_y) = 0: Ay = P + w * L - By = 54 + 6 * 6.9 - 44.96 = 50.44 kN

**Step 2:** Cut the beam at x = 1.6 m and take the left free body.
The forces crossing the cut are the reaction Ay and the distributed load over the length x only (the point load lies beyond the section, since x = 1.6 m < a = 3.1 m).

**Step 3:** Sum moments about the cut to find the internal moment (sagging positive).
M = Ay * x - w * x^2 / 2
M = 50.44 * 1.6 - 6 * (1.6)^2 / 2 = 73.02 kN*m

**Answer:** The internal bending moment at the section is 73.02 kN*m
