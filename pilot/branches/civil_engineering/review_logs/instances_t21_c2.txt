===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A rectangular open channel is 5.4 m wide and has a vitrified clay lining (Manning's n = 0.015). The channel carries uniform flow at a depth of 2.3 m on a longitudinal slope of S = 0.0017. Using Manning's equation in SI units, determine the discharge in m^3/s.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Width (b): 5.4 m; depth (y): 2.3 m
Slope (S): 0.0017; lining: vitrified clay, n = 0.015

**Step 1:** Compute the flow area and wetted perimeter.
A = b * y = 5.4 * 2.3 = 12.420 m^2
P = b + 2y = 5.4 + 2 * 2.3 = 10.00 m

**Step 2:** Compute the hydraulic radius.
R = A / P = 12.420 / 10.00 = 1.2420 m

**Step 3:** Evaluate the Manning terms.
R^(2/3) = (1.2420)^(2/3) = 1.1554
S^(1/2) = (0.0017)^(1/2) = 0.04123

**Step 4:** Apply Manning's equation.
Q = (1/n) * A * R^(2/3) * S^(1/2) = (1/0.015) * 12.420 * 1.1554 * 0.04123 = 39.44 m^3/s

**Answer:** The discharge is 39.44 m^3/s
