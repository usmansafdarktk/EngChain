===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A continuous beam ABC of constant EI rests on a pin support at A, a roller at B, and a roller at C. The two spans are equal: AB = BC = L = 4.7 m, so B is at the center. The beam carries a uniformly distributed load of 25 kN/m over both spans. Using the force method with the reaction at B as the redundant, determine the vertical reaction at support B in kN.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Equal spans: L = 4.7 m (total length 2L = 9.4 m); constant EI
Load: a uniformly distributed load of 25 kN/m over both spans

**Step 1:** Establish the degree of indeterminacy and release the redundant.
Three vertical reactions with two equilibrium equations (vertical force and moment) make the beam indeterminate to the first degree. Choose By as the redundant and remove support B; the released structure is a simply supported beam of span 2L.

**Step 2:** Deflection of the released beam at B under the real load.
The released structure is a simply supported beam of span 2L = 9.4 m under w = 25 kN/m; its midspan deflection is
delta_B0 = 5*w*(2L)^4 / (384*EI) = 5 * 25 * (9.4)^4 / 384 / EI = 2541.5/EI  (downward)

**Step 3:** Flexibility coefficient — deflection at B due to a unit upward load at B on the released beam.
delta_BB = (2L)^3 / (48*EI) = (9.4)^3 / 48 / EI = 17.304/EI  (per kN)

**Step 4:** Apply compatibility at B: the net deflection at the support must be zero.
delta_B0 - By * delta_BB = 0
By = delta_B0 / delta_BB = 2541.5 / 17.304 = 146.87 kN  (EI cancels)

**Step 5:** Check equilibrium for the end reactions.
Ay = Cy = (total load - By)/2 = (235.0 - 146.87) / 2 = 44.06 kN, both positive, confirming a consistent solution.

**Answer:** The vertical reaction at support B is 146.87 kN

===== SEED 102 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A continuous beam ABC of constant EI rests on a pin support at A, a roller at B, and a roller at C. The two spans are equal: AB = BC = L = 3.4 m, so B is at the center. The beam carries a concentrated load of 30 kN at the middle of each span. Using the force method with the reaction at B as the redundant, determine the vertical reaction at support B in kN.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Equal spans: L = 3.4 m (total length 2L = 6.8 m); constant EI
Load: a concentrated load of 30 kN at the middle of each span

**Step 1:** Establish the degree of indeterminacy and release the redundant.
Three vertical reactions with two equilibrium equations (vertical force and moment) make the beam indeterminate to the first degree. Choose By as the redundant and remove support B; the released structure is a simply supported beam of span 2L.

**Step 2:** Deflection of the released beam at B under the real loads.
The released structure is a simply supported beam of span 2L = 6.8 m carrying 30 kN at x = L/2 = 1.70 m and at x = 3L/2 = 5.10 m. Using the standard off-center-load formula delta_c = P*b*(3*(2L)^2 - 4*b^2)/(48*EI) with b = L/2 for each load and superposing the two equal contributions:
delta_B0 = 2 * 30 * 1.70 * (3 * (6.8)^2 - 4 * (1.70)^2) / 48 / EI = 270.2/EI  (downward)

**Step 3:** Flexibility coefficient — deflection at B due to a unit upward load at B on the released beam.
delta_BB = (2L)^3 / (48*EI) = (6.8)^3 / 48 / EI = 6.551/EI  (per kN)

**Step 4:** Apply compatibility at B: the net deflection at the support must be zero.
delta_B0 - By * delta_BB = 0
By = delta_B0 / delta_BB = 270.2 / 6.551 = 41.25 kN  (EI cancels)

**Step 5:** Check equilibrium for the end reactions.
Ay = Cy = (total load - By)/2 = (60.0 - 41.25) / 2 = 9.38 kN, both positive, confirming a consistent solution.

**Answer:** The vertical reaction at support B is 41.25 kN
