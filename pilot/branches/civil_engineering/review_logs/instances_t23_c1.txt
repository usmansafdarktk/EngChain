===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A rectangular channel lined with vitrified clay (Manning's n = 0.015) must carry a design discharge of 34.6 m^3/s on a slope of S = 0.0028. The channel is to be proportioned as the best hydraulic (most efficient) rectangular section, for which the width is twice the depth. Determine the required flow depth y in m.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Design discharge (Q): 34.6 m^3/s; slope (S): 0.0028; n = 0.015

**Step 1:** State the best hydraulic rectangular section properties.
For the most efficient rectangular section, b = 2y, so A = 2y^2, P = 4y, and R = A/P = y/2.

**Step 2:** Substitute into Manning's equation and collect powers of y.
Q = (1/n) * 2y^2 * (y/2)^(2/3) * S^(1/2) = 2^(1/3) * y^(8/3) * S^(1/2) / n, with 2^(1/3) = 1.2599 and S^(1/2) = 0.05292

**Step 3:** Solve for the depth.
y = [Q*n / (1.2599 * 0.05292)]^(3/8) = [34.6 * 0.015 / (1.2599 * 0.05292)]^(3/8) = 2.159 m
(width b = 2y = 4.318 m)

**Step 4:** Back-check with Manning's equation.
A = 2y^2 = 9.323 m^2, R = y/2 = 1.0795 m
Q = (1/0.015) * 9.323 * (1.0795)^(2/3) * 0.05292 = 34.61 m^3/s, matching the design discharge.

**Answer:** The required flow depth is 2.159 m
