===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A two-member steel bracket truss lies in a vertical plane. Joint A is pinned to a wall, joint B is pinned to the wall directly above A at height h = 1.8 m, and joint C is located a horizontal distance b = 3.2 m from A at the same level as A. Member AC is horizontal (cross-sectional area 840 mm^2) and member BC is the diagonal (area 1200 mm^2). A vertical downward load of P = 54 kN acts at joint C. Taking E = 200 GPa for both members, use the method of virtual work to determine the vertical deflection of joint C in mm.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Geometry: b = 3.2 m, h = 1.8 m; L_AC = 3.2 m, L_BC = sqrt(b^2 + h^2) = 3.672 m
Areas: A_AC = 840 mm^2, A_BC = 1200 mm^2; E = 200 GPa = 200 kN/mm^2
Load (P): 54 kN downward at C

**Step 1:** Real member forces (tension positive), from equilibrium of joint C.
Vertical: N_BC * (h / L_BC) = P  ->  N_BC = P * L_BC / h = 54 * 3.672 / 1.8 = 110.16 kN (tension)
Horizontal: N_AC = -N_BC * (b / L_BC) = -P * b / h = -54 * 3.2 / 1.8 = -96.00 kN (compression)

**Step 2:** Virtual member forces under a unit vertical downward load at C (so a positive result means a downward deflection); the joint equilibrium repeats with P = 1:
n_BC = L_BC / h = 3.672 / 1.8 = 2.0400
n_AC = -b / h = -3.2 / 1.8 = -1.7778

**Step 3:** Tabulate n*N*L/(A*E) for each member (L in mm).
BC: 2.0400 * 110.16 * 3672 / (1200 * 200) = 3.438 mm
AC: (-1.7778) * (-96.00) * 3200 / (840 * 200) = 3.251 mm

**Step 4:** Sum the contributions.
delta_C = 3.438 + 3.251 = 6.689 mm (downward)

**Answer:** The vertical deflection of joint C is 6.689 mm
