===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A soil profile consists of, from the ground surface down: 2.4 m of moist sand (unit weight 17.9 kN/m^3) above the water table; 2.6 m of saturated sand (saturated unit weight 19.7 kN/m^3); and a 4.9 m thick layer of normally consolidated clay (saturated unit weight 18.44 kN/m^3, initial void ratio 1.00, liquid limit 56%). The water table is at the bottom of the moist sand layer. A wide surcharge of 121 kPa is applied at the ground surface. Using the Skempton correlation Cc = 0.009(LL - 10) and taking the unit weight of water as 9.81 kN/m^3, determine the primary consolidation settlement of the clay layer in millimeters.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Moist sand: thickness z1 = 2.4 m, gamma = 17.9 kN/m^3
Saturated sand: thickness z2 = 2.6 m, gamma_sat = 19.7 kN/m^3
Clay: thickness Hc = 4.9 m, gamma_sat = 18.44 kN/m^3, e0 = 1.00, LL = 56%
Surcharge (delta_sigma): 121 kPa
Unit weight of water (gamma_w): 9.81 kN/m^3

**Step 1:** Estimate the compression index from the Skempton correlation.
Cc = 0.009 * (LL - 10) = 0.009 * (56 - 10) = 0.414

**Step 2:** Compute the submerged unit weights below the water table.
Sand: gamma'_sand = 19.7 - 9.81 = 9.89 kN/m^3
Clay: gamma'_clay = 18.44 - 9.81 = 8.63 kN/m^3

**Step 3:** Compute the initial effective stress at the middle of the clay layer.
sigma'0 = gamma_m * z1 + gamma'_sand * z2 + gamma'_clay * Hc/2
sigma'0 = 17.9 * 2.4 + 9.89 * 2.6 + 8.63 * 2.45 = 89.8 kPa

**Step 4:** Compute the final effective stress after the wide surcharge is applied.
sigma'f = sigma'0 + delta_sigma = 89.8 + 121 = 210.8 kPa

**Step 5:** Compute the logarithmic stress-ratio term.
log10(sigma'f / sigma'0) = log10(210.8 / 89.8) = 0.3706

**Step 6:** Compute the primary consolidation settlement.
Sc = Cc * Hc / (1 + e0) * log10(sigma'f/sigma'0) = 0.414 * 4.9 / (1 + 1.00) * 0.3706 = 0.3759 m

**Step 7:** Convert to millimeters.
Sc = 0.3759 * 1000 = 376 mm

**Answer:** The primary consolidation settlement is 376 mm
