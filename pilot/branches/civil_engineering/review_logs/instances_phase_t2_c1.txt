===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A natural deposit of clean sand has a field dry unit weight of 17.51 kN/m^3. Laboratory tests on the same sand give a maximum void ratio of 0.84 and a minimum void ratio of 0.42. The specific gravity of the soil solids is 2.66 and the unit weight of water is 9.81 kN/m^3. Determine the relative density of the deposit in percent.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Field dry unit weight (gamma_d): 17.51 kN/m^3
Maximum void ratio (e_max): 0.84
Minimum void ratio (e_min): 0.42
Specific gravity of solids (Gs): 2.66
Unit weight of water (gamma_w): 9.81 kN/m^3

**Step 1:** Compute the field void ratio from the dry unit weight.
From gamma_d = Gs * gamma_w / (1 + e), solving for e:
e = (Gs * gamma_w / gamma_d) - 1 = (2.66 * 9.81 / 17.51) - 1 = 0.490

**Step 2:** Compute the relative density.
Dr = (e_max - e) / (e_max - e_min) = (0.84 - 0.490) / (0.84 - 0.42) = 0.833

**Step 3:** Express as a percentage and classify.
Dr = 0.833 * 100 = 83.3%, so the deposit is in a dense state.

**Answer:** The relative density is 83.3 %
