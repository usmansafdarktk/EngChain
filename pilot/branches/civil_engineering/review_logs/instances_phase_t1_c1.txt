===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A moist sample of inorganic clay taken from a borrow area has a total (moist) unit weight of 17.19 kN/m^3 and a moisture content of 19.8%. The specific gravity of the soil solids is 2.78. Taking the unit weight of water as 9.81 kN/m^3, determine the degree of saturation of the sample in percent.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Moist unit weight (gamma): 17.19 kN/m^3
Moisture content (w): 19.8% = 0.198
Specific gravity of solids (Gs): 2.78
Unit weight of water (gamma_w): 9.81 kN/m^3

**Step 1:** Compute the dry unit weight.
The dry unit weight removes the weight of water from the total:
gamma_d = gamma / (1 + w) = 17.19 / (1 + 0.198) = 14.35 kN/m^3

**Step 2:** Compute the void ratio.
From gamma_d = Gs * gamma_w / (1 + e), solving for e:
e = (Gs * gamma_w / gamma_d) - 1 = (2.78 * 9.81 / 14.35) - 1 = 0.901

**Step 3:** Compute the degree of saturation.
Using the relation S * e = w * Gs:
S = w * Gs / e = (0.198 * 2.78) / 0.901 = 0.611 = 61.1%

**Answer:** The degree of saturation is 61.1 %
