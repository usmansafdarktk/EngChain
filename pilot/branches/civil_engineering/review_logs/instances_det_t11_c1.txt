===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A simply supported beam AB has a span of L = 7.5 m, with a pin support at A and a roller support at B. It carries a concentrated load of 22 kN at a distance of 5.6 m from A, together with a triangularly distributed load that varies from zero at A to 10 kN/m at B. Determine the vertical reaction at support A in kN.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Span (L): 7.5 m
Point load (P): 22 kN at a = 5.6 m from A
Distributed load: a triangularly distributed load that varies from zero at A to 10 kN/m at B

**Step 1:** Replace the distributed load by its resultant.
For a triangular load (zero at A, peak at B):
W = w_max * L / 2 = 10 * 7.5 / 2 = 37.5 kN, acting at two-thirds of the span from A, x_bar = 2L/3 = 5.00 m.

**Step 2:** Take moments about A to find the reaction at B.
Sum(M_A) = 0: By * L = P * a + W * x_bar
By = (22 * 5.6 + 37.5 * 5.00) / 7.5 = 41.43 kN

**Step 3:** Apply vertical force equilibrium to find the reaction at A.
Sum(F_y) = 0: Ay = P + W - By = 22 + 37.5 - 41.43 = 18.07 kN

**Answer:** The vertical reaction at support A is 18.07 kN

===== SEED 102 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A simply supported beam AB has a span of L = 4.9 m, with a pin support at A and a roller support at B. It carries a concentrated load of 49 kN at a distance of 2.0 m from A, together with a uniformly distributed load of 8 kN/m over the entire span. Determine the vertical reaction at support A in kN.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Span (L): 4.9 m
Point load (P): 49 kN at a = 2.0 m from A
Distributed load: a uniformly distributed load of 8 kN/m over the entire span

**Step 1:** Replace the distributed load by its resultant.
For a uniform load over the full span:
W = w * L = 8 * 4.9 = 39.2 kN, acting at the midspan, x_bar = L/2 = 2.45 m from A.

**Step 2:** Take moments about A to find the reaction at B.
Sum(M_A) = 0: By * L = P * a + W * x_bar
By = (49 * 2.0 + 39.2 * 2.45) / 4.9 = 39.60 kN

**Step 3:** Apply vertical force equilibrium to find the reaction at A.
Sum(F_y) = 0: Ay = P + W - By = 49 + 39.2 - 39.60 = 48.60 kN

**Answer:** The vertical reaction at support A is 48.60 kN
