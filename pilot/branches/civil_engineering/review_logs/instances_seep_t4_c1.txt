===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A constant-head permeability test is run on a specimen of coarse sand. The cylindrical specimen has a diameter of 7.9 cm and a length of 11.9 cm, and the head difference across the specimen is held constant at 60 cm. Over a period of 230 seconds, 4613.9 cm^3 of water is collected at the outlet. Determine the coefficient of permeability of the sand in cm/s.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Specimen diameter (D): 7.9 cm
Specimen length (L): 11.9 cm
Constant head difference (h): 60 cm
Collection time (t): 230 s
Collected volume (V): 4613.9 cm^3

**Step 1:** Compute the specimen cross-sectional area.
A = pi * D^2 / 4 = pi * (7.9)^2 / 4 = 49.02 cm^2

**Step 2:** Apply Darcy's law for the constant-head test.
For steady flow, V = k * i * A * t with i = h / L, which gives
k = V * L / (A * h * t)

**Step 3:** Substitute and evaluate.
k = (4613.9 * 11.9) / (49.02 * 60 * 230) = 8.116e-02 cm/s

**Answer:** The coefficient of permeability is 8.116e-02 cm/s
