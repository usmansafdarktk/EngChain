===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A rectangular channel with a ordinary concrete lining lining (Manning's n = 0.013) must carry a design discharge of 36.8 m^3/s on a slope of S = 0.0012. The channel is to be proportioned as the best hydraulic (most efficient) rectangular section, for which the width is twice the depth. Determine the required flow depth y in m.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Design discharge (Q): 36.8 m^3/s; slope (S): 0.0012; n = 0.013

**Step 1:** State the best hydraulic rectangular section properties.
For the most efficient rectangular section, b = 2y, so A = 2y^2, P = 4y, and R = A/P = y/2.

**Step 2:** Substitute into Manning's equation and collect powers of y.
Q = (1/n) * 2y^2 * (y/2)^(2/3) * S^(1/2) = 2^(1/3) * y^(8/3) * S^(1/2) / n, with 2^(1/3) = 1.2599 and S^(1/2) = 0.03464

**Step 3:** Solve for the depth.
y = [Q*n / (1.2599 * 0.03464)]^(3/8) = [36.8 * 0.013 / (1.2599 * 0.03464)]^(3/8) = 2.454 m
(width b = 2y = 4.908 m)

**Step 4:** Back-check with Manning's equation.
A = 2y^2 = 12.044 m^2, R = y/2 = 1.2270 m
Q = (1/0.013) * 12.044 * (1.2270)^(2/3) * 0.03464 = 36.78 m^3/s, matching the design discharge.

**Answer:** The required flow depth is 2.454 m
