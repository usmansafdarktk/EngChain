===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A strip footing 1.7 m wide is founded at a depth of 1.2 m in compacted clayey soil with unit weight 18.8 kN/m^3, effective cohesion c' = 20 kPa, and drained friction angle phi' = 25 degrees. The groundwater table is well below the footing. Using Terzaghi's bearing capacity equation for general shear failure with Nc = 25.13, Nq = 12.72, and Ngamma = 8.34, determine the allowable bearing capacity in kPa for a factor of safety of 3.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Footing width (B): 1.7 m, depth (Df): 1.2 m
Soil: gamma = 18.8 kN/m^3, c' = 20 kPa, phi' = 25 deg
Bearing capacity factors: Nc = 25.13, Nq = 12.72, Ngamma = 8.34
Factor of safety (FS): 3

**Step 1:** Compute the effective surcharge at the footing base.
q = gamma * Df = 18.8 * 1.2 = 22.56 kPa

**Step 2:** Evaluate the three terms of Terzaghi's equation for a strip footing.
Cohesion term: c' * Nc = 20 * 25.13 = 502.6 kPa
Surcharge term: q * Nq = 22.56 * 12.72 = 287.0 kPa
Width term: 0.5 * gamma * B * Ngamma = 0.5 * 18.8 * 1.7 * 8.34 = 133.3 kPa

**Step 3:** Sum the terms to obtain the ultimate bearing capacity.
qu = 502.6 + 287.0 + 133.3 = 922.9 kPa

**Step 4:** Apply the factor of safety.
q_all = qu / FS = 922.9 / 3 = 307.6 kPa

**Answer:** The allowable bearing capacity is 307.6 kPa
