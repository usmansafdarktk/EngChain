===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A Warren truss has bottom-chord joints A, C, and E at x = 0, 6.4 m, and 12.8 m along the base, and top-chord joints B and D at x = 3.2 m and 9.6 m, both at height h = 2.3 m. The members are AB, BC, CD, DE, the bottom chords AC and CE, and the top chord BD. The truss has a pin support at A, a roller support at E, and carries a single vertical downward load of P = 54 kN at joint C. Using the method of sections with a vertical cut between joints B and C (crossing members BD, BC, and AC), determine the magnitude of the force in member BD in kN.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Panel geometry: bottom joints at 0, 6.4, 12.8 m; top joints at 3.2, 9.6 m; height h = 2.3 m
Load (P): 54 kN downward at joint C
Requested member: BD

**Step 1:** Find the support reactions.
The load at C is at midspan, so by symmetry:
Ay = Ey = P / 2 = 54 / 2 = 27.0 kN

**Step 2:** Cut the truss between B and C.
The cut crosses the top chord BD, the diagonal BC, and the bottom chord AC. Consider the left free body, which carries the reaction Ay at A and the three unknown member forces.

**Step 3:** Take moments about joint C (at x = 2d on the base) for the left free body.
Only Ay and F_BD have moment about C (BC and AC pass through C):
F_BD * h = Ay * 2d
F_BD = Ay * 2d / h = 27.0 * 6.4 / 2.3 = 75.13 kN (compression)

**Answer:** The force in member BD is 75.13 kN (compression)

===== SEED 102 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A Warren truss has bottom-chord joints A, C, and E at x = 0, 4.6 m, and 9.2 m along the base, and top-chord joints B and D at x = 2.3 m and 6.9 m, both at height h = 2.2 m. The members are AB, BC, CD, DE, the bottom chords AC and CE, and the top chord BD. The truss has a pin support at A, a roller support at E, and carries a single vertical downward load of P = 30 kN at joint C. Using the method of sections with a vertical cut between joints B and C (crossing members BD, BC, and AC), determine the magnitude of the force in member BC in kN.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Panel geometry: bottom joints at 0, 4.6, 9.2 m; top joints at 2.3, 6.9 m; height h = 2.2 m
Load (P): 30 kN downward at joint C
Requested member: BC

**Step 1:** Find the support reactions.
The load at C is at midspan, so by symmetry:
Ay = Ey = P / 2 = 30 / 2 = 15.0 kN

**Step 2:** Cut the truss between B and C.
The cut crosses the top chord BD, the diagonal BC, and the bottom chord AC. Consider the left free body, which carries the reaction Ay at A and the three unknown member forces.

**Step 3:** Sum vertical forces on the left free body.
Chords AC and BD are horizontal, so the diagonal BC must carry the panel shear. Its length is L_BC = sqrt(d^2 + h^2) = sqrt(2.3^2 + 2.2^2) = 3.183 m, so its vertical component fraction is h / L_BC.
F_BC * (h / L_BC) = Ay
F_BC = Ay * L_BC / h = 15.0 * 3.183 / 2.2 = 21.70 kN (tension)

**Answer:** The force in member BC is 21.70 kN (tension)
