===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
An excavation is underlain by a 3.9 m thick layer of sand through which water seeps vertically upward. Piezometer readings show that the head loss across the layer is 1.48 m. The sand has a void ratio of 0.53 and a specific gravity of solids of 2.66, and the unit weight of water is 9.81 kN/m^3. Determine the factor of safety against the quick (boiling) condition.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Sand layer thickness (L): 3.9 m
Head loss across the layer (h): 1.48 m
Void ratio (e): 0.53
Specific gravity of solids (Gs): 2.66
Unit weight of water (gamma_w): 9.81 kN/m^3

**Step 1:** Compute the upward hydraulic gradient through the layer.
i = h / L = 1.48 / 3.9 = 0.3795

**Step 2:** Compute the submerged (effective) unit weight of the sand.
gamma' = (Gs - 1) * gamma_w / (1 + e) = (2.66 - 1) * 9.81 / (1 + 0.53) = 10.64 kN/m^3

**Step 3:** Compute the critical hydraulic gradient.
The quick condition occurs when the upward seepage force cancels the submerged weight:
i_cr = gamma' / gamma_w = 10.64 / 9.81 = 1.0846

**Step 4:** Compute the factor of safety.
FS = i_cr / i = 1.0846 / 0.3795 = 2.858

**Answer:** The factor of safety against the quick condition is 2.858
