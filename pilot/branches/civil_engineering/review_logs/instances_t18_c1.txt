===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A cantilever steel beam is built from a W24X76 section (SI designation W610X113; moment of inertia I = 874.0 x 10^6 mm^4) and extends L = 8.2 m from its fixed support at A. It carries a concentrated load of P = 32.9 kN at the free end. Taking E = 200 GPa, use the double-integration method to determine the deflection at the free end in mm.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Section: W24X76, I = 874.0 x 10^6 mm^4 = 0.000874 m^4
E = 200 GPa = 200000000 kN/m^2; length L = 8.2 m
Load: a concentrated load of P = 32.9 kN at the free end

**Step 1:** Write the internal moment function (x measured from the fixed end A).
M(x) = -P * (L - x) = -32.9 * (8.2 - x)

**Step 2:** Integrate E*I*v'' = M(x) once for the slope.
E*I*v' = -P*(L*x - x^2/2) + C1; the fixed end gives v'(0) = 0, so C1 = 0.

**Step 3:** Integrate again for the deflection.
E*I*v = -P*(L*x^2/2 - x^3/6) + C2; v(0) = 0 gives C2 = 0.

**Step 4:** Evaluate at the tip, x = L.
E*I*v(L) = -P*(L^3/2 - L^3/6) = -P*L^3/3, so the downward tip deflection is delta = P*L^3 / (3*E*I)

**Step 5:** Substitute numerical values.
delta = 32.9 * (8.2)^3 / (3 * 200000000 * 0.000874) = 0.03459 m

**Step 6:** Express in millimeters.
delta = 0.03459 * 1000 = 34.6 mm

**Answer:** The deflection at the free end is 34.6 mm

===== SEED 102 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A cantilever steel beam is built from a W12X26 section (SI designation W310X38.7; moment of inertia I = 84.9 x 10^6 mm^4) and extends L = 2.8 m from its fixed support at A. It carries a uniformly distributed load of w = 22.6 kN/m over its entire length. Taking E = 200 GPa, use the double-integration method to determine the deflection at the free end in mm.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Section: W12X26, I = 84.9 x 10^6 mm^4 = 0.000085 m^4
E = 200 GPa = 200000000 kN/m^2; length L = 2.8 m
Load: a uniformly distributed load of w = 22.6 kN/m over its entire length

**Step 1:** Write the internal moment function (x measured from the fixed end A).
M(x) = -w * (L - x)^2 / 2 = -22.6 * (2.8 - x)^2 / 2

**Step 2:** Integrate E*I*v'' = M(x) once for the slope.
E*I*v' = w*(L - x)^3/6 + C1; v'(0) = 0 gives C1 = -w*L^3/6.

**Step 3:** Integrate again for the deflection.
E*I*v = -w*(L - x)^4/24 - w*L^3*x/6 + C2; v(0) = 0 gives C2 = w*L^4/24.

**Step 4:** Evaluate at the tip, x = L.
E*I*v(L) = -w*L^4/6 + w*L^4/24 = -w*L^4/8, so the downward tip deflection is delta = w*L^4 / (8*E*I)

**Step 5:** Substitute numerical values.
delta = 22.6 * (2.8)^4 / (8 * 200000000 * 0.000085) = 0.01023 m

**Step 6:** Express in millimeters.
delta = 0.01023 * 1000 = 10.2 mm

**Answer:** The deflection at the free end is 10.2 mm
