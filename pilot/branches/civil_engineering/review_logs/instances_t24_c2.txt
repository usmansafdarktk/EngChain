===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
Uniform flow of Q = 17.71 m^3/s occurs in a rectangular channel of width b = 4.8 m, with a vitrified clay lining (Manning's n = 0.015) on a slope of S = 0.002. Determine the normal depth of flow in m as follows: form the required section factor K = Q*n/S^(1/2), define g(y) = A*R^(2/3) - K, evaluate g at trial depths of 1.000 m and 1.500 m, and then update the depth by linear interpolation (secant) between successive trials until the depth changes by less than 0.002 m.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Q = 17.71 m^3/s; a rectangular channel of width b = 4.8 m; n = 0.015; S = 0.002

**Step 1:** Form the required section factor from Manning's equation.
K = A*R^(2/3) = Q*n / S^(1/2) = 17.71 * 0.015 / 0.04472 = 5.940 m^(8/3)

**Step 2:** Set up the prescribed trial-and-interpolation scheme.
With A = b*y and P = b + 2y, the section factor A*R^(2/3) increases monotonically with depth. Evaluate g(y) = A*R^(2/3) - K at the trial depths 1.000 m and 1.500 m, then interpolate linearly between successive trials until the depth change is below 0.002 m.

**Step 3:** Iterate.
At y = 1.0000 m: A = 4.800 m^2, P = 6.800 m, A*R^(2/3) = 3.805, so g = 3.805 - 5.940 = -2.135
At y = 1.5000 m: A = 7.200 m^2, P = 7.800 m, A*R^(2/3) = 6.826, so g = 6.826 - 5.940 = 0.886
Update 1: y_next = 1.5000 - (0.886) * (1.5000 - 1.0000) / ((0.886) - (-2.135)) = 1.3534 m
At y = 1.3534 m: A = 6.496 m^2, P = 7.507 m, A*R^(2/3) = 5.899, so g = 5.899 - 5.940 = -0.041
Update 2: y_next = 1.3534 - (-0.041) * (1.3534 - 1.5000) / ((-0.041) - (0.886)) = 1.3599 m
At y = 1.3599 m: A = 6.528 m^2, P = 7.520 m, A*R^(2/3) = 5.940, so g = 5.940 - 5.940 = 0.000
Update 3: y_next = 1.3599 - (0.000) * (1.3599 - 1.3534) / ((0.000) - (-0.041)) = 1.3599 m

**Step 4:** State the converged normal depth.
The last change is below the tolerance, so yn = 1.360 m

**Answer:** The normal depth is 1.360 m
