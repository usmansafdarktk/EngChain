===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
At a site, a deep deposit of sand has its groundwater table at a depth of 3.4 m below the ground surface. Above the water table the sand is moist, with a dry unit weight of 15.66 kN/m^3 and a moisture content of 7.6%. Below the water table the sand is saturated, with a void ratio of 0.73 and a specific gravity of solids of 2.67. Taking the unit weight of water as 9.81 kN/m^3, determine the vertical effective stress in kPa at point A, located 6.3 m below the water table.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Depth to water table (z1): 3.4 m
Moist zone: gamma_d = 15.66 kN/m^3, w = 7.6% = 0.076
Saturated zone: e = 0.73, Gs = 2.67
Depth of A below water table (z2): 6.3 m
Unit weight of water (gamma_w): 9.81 kN/m^3

**Step 1:** Compute the moist unit weight above the water table.
gamma_moist = gamma_d * (1 + w) = 15.66 * (1 + 0.076) = 16.85 kN/m^3

**Step 2:** Compute the saturated unit weight below the water table.
gamma_sat = (Gs + e) * gamma_w / (1 + e) = (2.67 + 0.73) * 9.81 / (1 + 0.73) = 19.28 kN/m^3

**Step 3:** Compute the total vertical stress at A.
sigma = gamma_moist * z1 + gamma_sat * z2 = 16.85 * 3.4 + 19.28 * 6.3 = 178.8 kPa

**Step 4:** Compute the pore water pressure at A.
u = gamma_w * z2 = 9.81 * 6.3 = 61.8 kPa

**Step 5:** Apply the effective stress principle.
sigma' = sigma - u = 178.8 - 61.8 = 117.0 kPa

**Answer:** The vertical effective stress at point A is 117.0 kPa
