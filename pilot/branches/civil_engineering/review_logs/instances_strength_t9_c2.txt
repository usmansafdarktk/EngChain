===== SEED 101 =====
--- QUESTION ---
A natural slope in dry, cohesionless sand (sand, rounded, dense) is inclined at 24.7 degrees to the horizontal. The drained friction angle of the soil is 37 degrees, and there is no seepage. Using the infinite-slope model, determine the factor of safety against sliding.
--- GOLD SOLUTION ---
**Given:**
Slope angle (beta): 24.7 deg
Drained friction angle (phi'): 37 deg
Soil: dry, cohesionless (c' = 0), no seepage

**Step 1:** Select the governing relation.
For an infinite slope in dry cohesionless soil, the sliding resistance is purely frictional, and the factor of safety is independent of depth and unit weight:
FS = tan(phi') / tan(beta)

**Step 2:** Evaluate the two tangents.
tan(phi') = tan(37 deg) = 0.7536
tan(beta) = tan(24.7 deg) = 0.4599

**Step 3:** Compute the factor of safety.
FS = 0.7536 / 0.4599 = 1.639

**Answer:** The factor of safety against sliding is 1.639
